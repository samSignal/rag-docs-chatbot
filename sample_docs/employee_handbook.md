# Northwind Tech (fictional) - Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Standard working hours are 08:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees may work flexible hours between 07:00 and 19:00 provided they complete 40 hours per week and attend core meetings.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their three-month probation period. Remote staff must be reachable on the team chat during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00. Fully remote contracts require approval from a department head.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried over to the next year and must be used before 31 March. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days.

## Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop and a monitor. Remote workers may claim up to USD 300 once for home-office furniture. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT support within 24 hours.

## Training budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of USD 1,000 for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests are approved by the line manager.
