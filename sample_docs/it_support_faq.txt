Northwind Tech (fictional) - IT Support FAQ

How do I reset my password?
Go to the self-service portal and choose "Forgot password". A reset link is sent to your registered phone number. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are changed every 180 days.

How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the company VPN client from the software centre, sign in with your work account and approve the multi-factor authentication prompt. The VPN is required to reach internal databases and the HR portal from outside the office.

How do I report a phishing email?
Use the "Report phishing" button in your email client. Do not click links or open attachments in the suspicious message. The security team reviews every report within four hours.

How do I get software installed?
Request software through the service desk. Approved software is installed within two working days. Software that stores company data must be reviewed by the security team first.

What are the IT support hours?
The service desk is open from 07:30 to 18:00 on weekdays. For urgent outages outside these hours, call the on-call engineer.
